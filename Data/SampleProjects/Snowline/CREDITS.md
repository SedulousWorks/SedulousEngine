# Snowline - Credits

Made with the Sedulous engine. Third-party assets, with thanks:

## Made for this game

- The rider on the board (model, rig and animations), the trees, rocks, gates, finish, gems and
  kicker are built for Snowline from Blender scripts (`Tools/blender/`); the mountains, their
  textures and the course lines are generated by `Tools/terrain.py`; the wind, the gem chime and
  the avalanche's rumble are generated by `Tools/sounds.py`. All under the project's own licence.

## Sound effects

- **Kenney** ([kenney.nl](https://kenney.nl)), [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
  (`Licenses/Kenney-CC0.txt`): Impact Sounds (the board's pop, landing, crash and a hit on a tree),
  Interface Sounds (a gate passed and missed, the menus' click and back) and Music Jingles (the
  medal and no-medal stings). Renamed for what they do in the game; otherwise unmodified.
