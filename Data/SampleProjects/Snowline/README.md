# Snowline

A snowboard time trial with tricks, built through the engine's MCP tools, using terrain,
vegetation, splines, an animation graph driven by parameters, decals and physics joints. It is
Raptor's Snowline, ported: the same courses, models, sounds and rules, its scripts in this
engine's AngelScript API.

## The game

Ride a board down a mountain against the clock. Pass each slalom gate on its side (round the
outside costs two seconds), take the gems off the safe line, and hit the kickers for air: spin
with the stick and hold a grab, then land with the board along your path for a burst of speed;
land across it and you crash. Clean landings, near misses past trees and cleared gaps grow a
combo that multiplies the trick points. Medal ghosts ride the course at each medal's pace, and
your best run rides beside you as your own ghost.

Three courses, each opened by a bronze on the one before it:
- **Meadow**: open slopes and wide gates, sparse pines, one kicker.
- **Forest**: tighter bends, narrow gates, trees close on both sides (solid, and worth a near miss
  when passed close and fast), and a shortcut through them.
- **Ridge**: a long run between rocky sides, a run of three kickers, a gap over a crevasse, and an
  avalanche that breaks loose behind you on the last stretch, its distance on the HUD. Fall short
  of the gap or let the avalanche catch you and you crash.

A run ends with its results, a row at a time: the ride, the gates, the gems, the tricks and the
time bonus, then the final time with its medal and the score. Best times, medals and your ghost
are saved. Winning a better medal on Ridge brings up the ending: the best on every course.

Controls: A and D (or the left stick) to carve, and in the air to spin; Space to jump, Left Shift
to tuck, E to grab, Escape to pause (resume, restart the run or back to the courses). On a
gamepad: A (cross) to jump, X (square) or the right shoulder to tuck, B (circle) to grab, Start
to pause. The arrows and Enter, or the stick and A, drive the menus.

## Project layout

- `Project.xml`: the manifest: the start scene (`Scenes/Meadow`), the Game script
  (`Scripts/Snowline`) and the input map.
- `Sources/`: the raw sources the assets import from: the AngelScript scripts, the UI markup, the
  generated terrain images and textures, the models and the sounds.
- `Content/`: the asset envelopes (`*.xasset`) and their sidecars: the three course scenes and
  their terrains, the prefabs (gates, gems, the finish, the track marks), the rider with its
  skeleton, clips and animation graph, the materials, the particle effects, the sounds and the UI.
- `Tools/`: the authoring scripts that build the game through the editor's MCP tools: the
  terrain, the courses, the models, the effects and sounds, the thumbnails. Not game content;
  `Tools/README.md` says what each does.
- `export_presets.xml`: the export targets (Linux desktop, web, Steam Deck).
- `CREDITS.md` and `Licenses/`: the music, the sound effects and the font.
- `Cooked/`, `.cache/`, `Editor/`, `Dist/`: generated, and gitignored; the tools rebuild them.

The terrain, its textures and the ambient sounds are generated by `Tools/`, and the models are built
by its Blender scripts. The music (RaisinRiot, CC BY 3.0; Joth, CC0) and the sound effects
(Kenney, CC0) are credited in `CREDITS.md`, their licences in `Licenses/`; the UI font is Roboto
(Apache 2.0).
