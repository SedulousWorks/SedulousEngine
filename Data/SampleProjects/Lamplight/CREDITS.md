# Lamplight - Credits

Made with SedulousEngine. Third-party assets, with thanks:

## Made for this game

- Every model: the thief and the guards, the manor's pieces and furniture, the cellars, the grounds
  (hedges, walls, gates, lantern posts) and the loot, built for Lamplight from Blender scripts
  (`Tools/blender/`).
- Every sound (footsteps, rain, coins), generated by `Tools/sounds.py`.

All under the project's own licence.
